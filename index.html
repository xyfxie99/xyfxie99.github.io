On the 2026–2027 academic job market

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Agricultural and Resource Economics at the University of California, Davis, and expect to complete my degree in June 2027. My dissertation committee is Jens Hilscher (Co-Chair), Bulat Gafarov (Co-Chair), and Kristin Kiesel. My research is in food and agricultural economics, consumer behavior, and applied microeconomics.

I use large household scanner data to study how households respond to food prices and health information, and how these responses differ by income. My job market paper, joint with Bulat Gafarov and Jens Hilscher, studies cheapflation and the sluggish substitution between budget and premium grocery products.
